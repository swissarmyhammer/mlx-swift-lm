Found on 2026-08-14 by a `/test` pass on the `catch-up-upstream` branch.

`Tests/MLXGuidedGenerationTests/ConstraintCachingTests.swift` skips four tests.
The reason it gives is that the vendored `xgrammar` library needs v0.1.34, and
the vendored copy is v0.1.30.

## The history

- Commit `f635a2c` bumped the vendored `xgrammar` to v0.1.34, wrote a real
  `xg_matcher_fork` in `shim.cc` in place of a stub, and removed the skip from
  these four tests. It also removed the same skip from `testForkDiverges` of
  `IntegrationTesting/.../Grammar/ForkIndependenceTests.swift`.
- Commit `f1573a9`, the FoundationModels merge, put the v0.1.30 copy back. The
  revert was an accident of the merge, not a decision.

## Why it is not a small correction

To redo the bump, about 7,500 lines of third-party C++ must be vendored again
through `scripts/sync-xgrammar-source.sh v0.1.34`. That is too large for a
test-fix pass, thus a person must say whether to redo it.
