title: 'Qwen3.8 MTP verify attention: size blocks for long context like decode attention'
---
## Why
From 2k to 64k, a 4-token verify grows by 6.2 ms, about twice the KV-read floor. Plain decode grows by 3.2 ms, which is at the floor. Verify attention uses at most 16 blocks × 4 KV heads, which is 64 threadgroups. Decode attention uses 32–128 blocks of 128 keys.

## What
In `VerifyAttentionKernel.swift`, choose the split-K block count from the context length in the same way as the decode attention kernel. That gives more threadgroups at long context and a cheaper reduce.
