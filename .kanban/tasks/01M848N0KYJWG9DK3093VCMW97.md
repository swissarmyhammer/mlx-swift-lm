## Why
The ^pxcccjs attribution: with attention, MoE and lm_head all skipped, a decode step still costs 2.9 ms/token while moving almost no bytes. That remainder is the depth mix (×2 per visit), the message controllers (24 visits) and the convolution states. `inkling2DepthMix` puts one row in one threadgroup, so a decode step (1 row) runs it on a single threadgroup, twice in each of the 48 visits.

## What
- Sub-attribute the 2.9 ms between depth mix, message controllers and the rest, with skips as in scratchpad/ablation.patch.
- Split the depth mix of one row across threadgroups: by block or by channel slice, then a small reduce. Or fold the MLP depth mix into the router logits kernel, which reads its float32 copy.
