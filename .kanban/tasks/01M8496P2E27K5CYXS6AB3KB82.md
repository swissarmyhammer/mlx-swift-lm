## Why
^xdrv99b profiled Inkling decode, which makes ~430 custom-kernel calls per token. For every call, mlx (mlx-swift 0.33.0):
- `fast::metal_kernel`'s closure runs `write_signature`, which builds header + source + signature into a new string, several KB per call.
- `CustomKernel::eval_gpu` computes `std::hash<std::string>{}(source_)` over that whole string, then `fmt::format`s the library name, on every encode.

Together they take ~15–20% of the decode main thread, and every model with custom kernels pays them.

## What
- In the `metal_kernel` closure, memoize `kernel_name` and `kernel_source` by the template args and the input/output dtype signature. A small map in the closure, behind a mutex.
- In `CustomKernel`, compute `lib_name` once at construction.
- Measure with a locally patched mlx-swift checkout: `swift package edit`, or a path override in inklingcli.
- Write up the result and a draft upstream PR for the user. Do not open the PR.
