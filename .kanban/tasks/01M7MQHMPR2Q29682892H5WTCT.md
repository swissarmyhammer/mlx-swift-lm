    ## Profile (2026-11-17), Qwen3.8-27B-4bit, M5 Ultra, worktree build of stable f03e522f

    **Where the fixed KV8 cost was.** Two measurements:
    1. The update on its own (16 layers, 4 kv heads x 256, sync eval each step): bf16 `KVCacheSimple.update` takes 0.285 ms per step at 2k (1 row) and 0.258 ms (4 rows). `QuantizedKVCache.updateQuantized` takes 0.533 ms and 0.650 ms. Graph build is 0.07 ms against 0.19 ms. At 64k the two are level: bf16 0.79 ms, KV8 0.69 ms. So the update is at most about +0.25 to +0.4 ms in isolation, and only at short context.
    2. The real cause: `CompiledDecodeSchedule.caches` refused every `QuantizedKVCacheProtocol` cache (`usesPlainAttentionCacheRoute`). KV8 decode and verify therefore lost the cross-layer compiled segments and fell back to the per-layer general path. An env-toggled experiment let quantized caches into the schedule (median of 2 quiet runs). Plain 2k was bf16 17.12–17.28, KV8 17.55–17.70, KV8+schedule 17.19–17.23. Plain 32k was 19.78–19.83, 20.15–20.16 and 19.69–19.73. MTP 2k was 9.52, 10.18–10.60 and 9.79–9.96. MTP 32k was 12.50, 13.15–13.24 and 12.98–13.11. The schedule removes the plain fixed cost entirely. The MTP gap left at 2k comes from tokens/verify, 3.21 vs 3.29: KV8 numerics, not a per-round cost (ms per round is 31.7 vs 31.3). At 32k it is the KV8 verify attention kernel: about +1.5 ms per round, which grows with context. The update is not the cause.

## Why
^6xcb05k added fused 8-bit-KV decode/verify attention kernels. KV8 is now within 1-7% of bf16 KV but still not faster: the attention read saves only ~0.6 ms per plain step at 64k, and KV8 pays a context-independent ~0.4-0.7 ms per step (2k: plain +0.38 ms/tok, MTP +0.66 ms/tok vs bf16). The suspected source is `QuantizedKVCache.updateQuantized`: per layer per step it runs `quantized()` on the new rows, writes 6 slices (weights/scales/biases for K and V) and returns 6 slices, x16 full-attention layers. Numbers: comments of ^6xcb05k, scratchpad qwen_bench_kv8_6xcb05k.txt / qwen_bench_bf16_6xcb05k.txt.

## What
- Profile one KV8 decode step to confirm where the fixed cost goes.
- If it is the update: a fused quantize-and-write kernel for the new rows (K and V in one dispatch), or fewer graph nodes per update.

## Acceptance
- [ ] KV8 plain and MTP decode at 32k and 64k faster than bf16 KV (qwenbench.sh with QWEN_KV_BITS=8), or a recorded NO-GO.
