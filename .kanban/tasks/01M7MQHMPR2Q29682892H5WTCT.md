## Why
^6xcb05k added fused 8-bit-KV decode/verify attention kernels. KV8 is now within 1-7% of bf16 KV but still not faster: the attention read saves only ~0.6 ms per plain step at 64k, and KV8 pays a context-independent ~0.4-0.7 ms per step (2k: plain +0.38 ms/tok, MTP +0.66 ms/tok vs bf16). The suspected source is `QuantizedKVCache.updateQuantized`: per layer per step it runs `quantized()` on the new rows, writes 6 slices (weights/scales/biases for K and V) and returns 6 slices, x16 full-attention layers. Numbers: comments of ^6xcb05k, scratchpad qwen_bench_kv8_6xcb05k.txt / qwen_bench_bf16_6xcb05k.txt.

## What
- Profile one KV8 decode step to confirm where the fixed cost goes.
- If it is the update: a fused quantize-and-write kernel for the new rows (K and V in one dispatch), or fewer graph nodes per update.

## Acceptance
- [ ] KV8 plain and MTP decode at 32k and 64k faster than bf16 KV (qwenbench.sh with QWEN_KV_BITS=8), or a recorded NO-GO.
- [ ] Default path unchanged (`qwencheck.sh` identical). #qwen-perf-candidate
