## Why
Each of the 16 attention layers rebuilds the rotation on every decode token: per token that is 32 Cos/Sin, 80 Concatenate, 48 AsType and 32 Negative operations. Verify already computes it once and shares it. Flint commit fc40251a (FlintDecodeHeadsKernel.swift) fused the q/k norm, RoPE and head layout into one decode kernel, and its output was unchanged.

## What
- Compute the M-RoPE cos/sin once per decode step and share it across layers, as verify does.
- Add a fused decode-heads Metal kernel for Qwen35 (`projectPreRope` / `attend`): q/k RMSNorm, then M-RoPE, then the head layout. Write it in the style of FlintDecodeHeadsKernel.swift, and reuse MetalSimdWidth.swift.
- The kernel must repeat the float operations of the current path exactly. Keep the unfused path as the fallback for prefill and verify, and for any shape the kernel doesn't support.
