- `second.cachedTokenCount` was 0, against a ledger of 206.

What the log shows:
- The MTP session committed its turn-1 entry at 14:55:05 (ledger 206).
- `MTPGenerationTests` finished at about 14:55:11 and calls `mtpModel.evict()`. That reaches `ExecutorPromptCacheStore.evict(modelID:)`, which removes every entry for `mlx-community/Qwen3.8-27B-4bit`.
- At 14:55:13 the turn-2 plan found `source=none ... rule=cold`.

Run alone, the same suite passes (MTP turn 2 recomputes 26 of 233). Swift Testing runs separate suites in parallel, and `.serialized` only orders tests within a single suite.

Fix options: make the FM MTP suites share a serialized parent suite or a lock, or have each test evict only its own sessions (`evict(sessionID:)`) instead of the whole model id.
