## Why
The profile (2026-11-16) shows about 1160 kernels per decode token, 353 of them quantized matmuls. The k and v projections are 2.9 MB each and run at only about 300 GB/s. Flint commit 5b0b9e46 fused qkv and gate_up at load (FlintModel.prepareCheckpoint) and its output was bit-identical.

## What
In `Libraries/MLXVLM/Models/Qwen35.swift`, and in the MLXLLM twin where it is shared, concatenate at load:
- q/k/v for the 16 full-attention layers;
- gate/up for the 64 MLP layers.

Only fuse where the bits and group sizes match. Split the outputs after the matmul. Keep loading of unfused checkpoints working, and don't break the MTP drafter or LoRA paths.
