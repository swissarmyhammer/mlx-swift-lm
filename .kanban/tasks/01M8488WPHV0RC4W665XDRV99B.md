## Why
The ^pxcccjs attribution: the decode attention kernel costs 1.55 ms/token at 2k and 2.90 at 32k, against bandwidth floors of ~0.35 and ~1.8 ms.
- `inkling2DecodeAttention` runs the sliding visits (≤1024 keys, 40 of 48) as one threadgroup per query head: 24 threadgroups, while the M5 Ultra has many more cores.
- The split path (full visits past 1024 keys) uses 256 keys per split, so it launches only 8×S/256 threadgroups.

## What
- Microbenchmark first: set `singleThreadgroupDecodeKeys` and `keysPerSplit` (and the simdgroups) by env in a scratch build, and run inklingcli profile/bench at 2k and 32k.
- Candidates:
  - (a) Send sliding visits down the split path with small splits (64 or 128 keys), so there are ≥128 threadgroups.
  - (b) Choose the split count by the key count, aiming at a fixed threadgroup count.
- The verify rows (`causalRows`) share the function, so a verify pass keeps the bits of decode steps.
