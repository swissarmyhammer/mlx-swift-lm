## Why
Prefill falls from 4725 tok/s at 2k to 3585 at 32k and 2732 at 64k. Only the 8 full-attention visits grow with the context. `inkling2RelativeAttention` uses a query block of 32 (`relativeAttentionQueryBlock`) and 128 threads (`relativeAttentionThreads`), and it may also apply the sliding-window bound inefficiently on full layers.

## What
- Microbenchmark `inkling2RelativeAttention` at a 2048-query chunk against 8k, 32k and 64k keys.
- Sweep the query block (32 or 64), the threads, the key tile, and the K/V reuse across the query heads of one KV head.
- Compare the result to `MLXFast.scaledDotProductAttention` at the same shapes as an upper bound on speed. It has no relative bias, so it is a reference only.
