## Why
`Inkling2.prefillStepSize` is 2048, chosen so that each of the 128 experts gets enough rows. Qwen settled on 2048 (276947b4), and later had to cap its step below the MLX dsplit attention kernel (74e499b5). Inkling has its own relative attention kernel, so its best step may differ.

## What
- Pass `prefillStepSize` through GenerateParameters in inklingcli, so a sweep needs no library change.
- Sweep 1024, 2048, 3072 and 4096 at 2k, 8k, 32k and 64k.
- Watch peak memory too.
