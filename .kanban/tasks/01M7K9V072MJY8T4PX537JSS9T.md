## Why
Found while doing ^237qeb2. `LanguageModel.defaultPrefillStepSize` now carries a model's own prefill step into `PromptCacheCheckpoint.prefill`, the feed `ChatSession` and the FoundationModels executor run up to each message boundary. `Gemma3TextModel.prepare` (Libraries/MLXLLM/Models/Gemma3Text.swift) prefills at its own `defaultPrefillChunkSize` (128) and caps the step at `config.slidingWindow`, but it does not override `defaultPrefillStepSize`, so its checkpoint feeds (it has rotating caches, so checkpoints apply) still run at the generic 512 with no window cap.

## What
- Decide whether Gemma3Text should give `defaultPrefillStepSize` (128) and whether the checkpoint feed needs a maximum step (the sliding window) the way `prepare` caps it — for example a second `LanguageModel` property for the maximum step.
- Measure before and after on a Gemma 3 ChatSession prompt.

## Acceptance
- [ ] A unit test shows the Gemma3Text checkpoint feed step that the decision picks.
