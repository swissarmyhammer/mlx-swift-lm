## What

Path gap. The parts are covered one at a time:
- `renderTokens` survives the file: `Tests/MLXFoundationModelsTests/ExecutorPromptCacheFileTests.swift:248-249`.
- A protocol rule splices from an in-memory entry: `Tests/MLXFoundationModelsTests/ExecutorPromptCacheTests.swift:738-778`.
- `QwenCommittedTurnRule` alone: `Tests/MLXLMTests/QwenCommittedTurnRuleTests.swift:60-127`. It declines when no render is on record (168-174).
- `ExecutorPromptCacheSlot.restoreIfPending(model:parameters:)` (`Libraries/MLXFoundationModels/ExecutorPromptCache.swift:1653-1666`): 100%, but only the test at `ExecutorPromptCacheRestoreTests.swift:121-143` plans after a restore, and it plans an `extend`, not a `splice`.

No unit test proves the whole chain: an entry with a committed Qwen turn goes to disk, comes back through the slot, and the next plan is `.splice` with the reuse count of the committed turn. When `renderTokens` is lost in the file, a hybrid model cannot rewind and the turn rebuilds cold, thus this is the Qwen agent loop case.

What to test:
- Make an entry whose `tokens` are a render plus a generated turn that ends with the Qwen end-of-turn token, and whose `renderTokens` is that render. Use the fixture style of `QwenCommittedTurnRuleTests` and `QwenCommittedTurnRule(endOfTurnToken:)`.
- Check the entry into a store with memory budget 0, and wait for the spill. Check out: expect `.spilled`.
- Make an `ExecutorPromptCacheSlot(checkout, key:)`, call `restoreIfPending`, and call `plan(input:model:parameters:protocolRules: [rule], decodeTokens:)` with a next render that rewrites the model turn and adds a tool response after the commit.
- Expect `plan.decision == .splice`, `plan.reusedTokenCount` equal to the committed prefix, `plan.representedTokens` equal to the model tokens plus the fed tail, and a plan line that names `source=disk` and `rule=splice`.
- Control: the same chain with an entry whose `renderTokens` is empty gives a rule other than `splice`.
